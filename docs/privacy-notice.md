# Privacy notice templates

Under GDPR, a restaurant must tell people what it records about them, why, and for how long. This page has two notices to fill in: one for **staff** and one for **customers**. They are written from what Kishtin actually keeps, listed in [What Kishtin records](recording.md).

**These are templates, not legal advice.** Kishtin's authors are not lawyers. The reasons given for each use of data ("legal basis") are a sensible starting point for an Irish restaurant, not a ruling: check them with your solicitor, your accountant, or the [Data Protection Commission's guidance for organisations](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/organisations). The notices cover only Kishtin. Your payroll, rota, CCTV, booking system and till have their own.

How to use them:

1. Replace everything in **[square brackets]**.
2. Delete the lines marked *(only if…)* that do not apply to you. Check the settings they mention in **Settings**, so the notice says what your Kishtin really does.
3. Give the staff notice to every member of staff, and to new staff when they start. Put the customer notice where customers can read it: your website, or a printed copy they can ask for.
4. When you change a setting that changes what is kept (voice recordings, how long customer details are kept), update the notice.

## Staff privacy notice

> ### How [Restaurant name] uses your information in Kishtin
>
> **Who we are.** [Legal name of the business], [address]. We decide how your information is used (we are the "data controller"). Questions: [name or role], [email or phone].
>
> **What Kishtin is.** Kishtin is the kitchen system we use for food safety records, orders and the pass. It runs on a computer in the restaurant. Your information stays on that computer and on the backups we keep of it. It is not sent to a cloud service or to the makers of Kishtin.
>
> **What we keep about you**
>
> - Your name, your role, and whether your account is active.
> - Your PIN [and password], stored scrambled so nobody can read them, including us.
> - The food safety checks you log or are named on: what the check was, the reading, when, and any corrective action.
> - Training we record for you: topics or courses, the trainer and the date.
> - What you do on the kitchen screens: orders and items you fire, start, finish or void, dishes you mark sold out, and when you log in and out and on which device.
> - Voice commands you give to Kishtin: the words as Kishtin heard them, what it took them to mean, and what it did. Speech that does not start with the wake word is ignored and not kept.
> - *(only if Settings › "Keep recordings of voice commands" is above 0)* A recording of each voice command, kept for [number] days and then deleted. Only a manager with a password can play one.
> - *(only if we log fitness to work)* The outcome of a fitness-to-work assessment: the date, the reason for it, whether you were fit to work, and what was done. We do **not** keep symptoms, diagnoses or your answers to health questions in Kishtin. Only managers can see these records.
>
> **Why we keep it, and what allows us to**
>
> - **Food safety records** (checks, training, fitness to work): food law requires us to keep records showing our food safety procedures are followed, and to show them to environmental health officers. *Legal basis: a legal obligation. For fitness to work, which is information about health: our obligations as an employer and food business.*
> - **Running the kitchen** (orders, the pass, logins): to run service, and to see what happened when something goes wrong. *Legal basis: our legitimate interests in running the business safely and well.*
> - **Voice commands**: so you can work hands-free, and so a mistaken command can be traced. *Legal basis: our legitimate interests.* [If you record audio, say why: for example, to check what was said and to improve recognition of our own kitchen's voices.]
>
> We do not use this information to make automated decisions about you.
>
> **Who sees it**
>
> - Managers and the head chef, as their job needs.
> - Environmental health officers, when they inspect our food safety records.
> - [Anyone who maintains the computer for you, for example your IT support.]
> - Nobody else. It is not sold or shared, and it does not leave Ireland [or: the EU].
>
> **How long we keep it**
>
> - Food safety records, including your name on them: [number] years. They cannot be changed or deleted in Kishtin, because inspectors must be able to trust them.
> - When you leave, we deactivate your account. [After (period)] we replace your name with "Former staff" in the order, voice and activity logs, and remove your PIN and password. Your name stays on the food safety records you logged.
> - Voice recordings, if we keep them: [number] days.
> - Backups of the Kishtin database are kept for [period] and hold the same information.
>
> **Your rights.** You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it or to stop using it where the law allows, and object to uses based on our legitimate interests. We cannot delete food safety records we are required to keep; a mistake in one is fixed by adding a correction. To use any of these rights, contact [name or role]. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, [dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie/).
>
> Last updated: [date].

## Customer privacy notice

> ### How [Restaurant name] uses your information in our kitchen system
>
> **Who we are.** [Legal name of the business], [address]. Questions: [email or phone].
>
> **What we keep, and why**
>
> - **Your name on an order**, if you give one, for example for a takeaway or a booking, so we can call your order and hand it to the right person.
> - **Notes on your order**, such as an allergy or a dietary need, so the kitchen prepares your food safely.
> - *(only if you deliver food to customers)* **Your name on a delivery record**, with what was delivered, when and at what temperature, because food law requires us to keep records of food we send out.
>
> An allergy is information about your health. We use it only to prepare your order safely, because you told us for that purpose.
>
> We do not keep your card details in our kitchen system: payments are handled by [Square / SumUp / your payment provider], under their own privacy notice.
>
> **Where it is kept.** On a computer in our restaurant, and on the backups we keep of it. It is not sent to a cloud service by our kitchen system. [If orders come from your till: your order comes to the kitchen from our till, (Square / SumUp).]
>
> **How long we keep it.** Your name and your order's notes are removed from our kitchen system [30] days after your order is closed. *(Check this against Settings › "Clear customers' names and order notes"; if it is 0, they are kept, and you must say for how long and why.)* Backups made before then hold them for up to [period]. Delivery records are food safety records and are kept for [number] years.
>
> **What allows us to.** Preparing the order you asked for, and our legal obligations under food safety law. For allergies and dietary needs: your telling us so that we can prepare your food safely.
>
> **Your rights.** You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us at [email or phone]. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission, [dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie/).
>
> Last updated: [date].

## What to check before you publish

| The notice says | Check in Kishtin |
|---|---|
| Voice recordings are, or are not, kept, and for how many days | **Settings › Keep recordings of voice commands** (0 keeps none) |
| Customer names and notes are removed after 30 days | **Settings › Clear customers' names and order notes** |
| How long backups are kept | **Settings › Backups to keep**, and how long you keep the copies you take off the computer |
| Leavers are anonymised | That you do it: **Setup › Staff and access › Staff › Anonymise** ([Leavers](recording.md#leavers)) |
| Fitness to work is, or is not, logged in Kishtin | Whether you use that record type at all |
| How long food safety records are kept | Your HACCP plan, and what your environmental health officer asks for |

## If someone asks for their information

Kishtin has no single "export everything about this person" button. For now:

- **Staff**: their records are in **HACCP › Records** (search by their name) and the CSV export; what they did on the screens is in the activity log (`kishtin activity`) and each order's history.
- **Customers**: their name shows on their order in **Orders** (or `kishtin order list`) for the days you keep it. Kishtin has no search by customer name, so look at the day they ordered. After the days you keep it, their name is gone from Kishtin, and the honest answer is that you no longer hold it there.

You normally have one month to answer a request.

## If the computer is lost, or the data is seen by someone who should not

See [If the computer is lost or stolen](security.md#if-the-computer-is-lost-or-stolen). You may have to tell the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours, and the people affected.
