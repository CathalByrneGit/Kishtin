The first release of Kishtin, a kitchen system that runs on one computer in your restaurant, with no cloud account and no subscription.

**New to Kishtin?** Start with the [getting-started guide](https://github.com/cathalbyrnegit/kishtin/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md). It takes about an hour, outside service.

## Install

**Windows**: open PowerShell and paste:

```powershell
irm https://github.com/cathalbyrnegit/kishtin/releases/latest/download/install.ps1 | iex
```

**Mac or Linux**: open Terminal and paste:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://github.com/cathalbyrnegit/kishtin/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh
```

The installer checks the download against `SHA256SUMS` before installing anything, and offers to start Kishtin with the computer. Then open http://127.0.0.1:8080 on that computer to set it up.

## What's in it

- **HACCP log**: fridge and freezer checks, deliveries (with a photo of the docket), cooking, cooling, hot holding, batch labels and cleaning, checked against the FSAI's limits. A failed reading needs a corrective action. Records cannot be edited or deleted, and each is chained to the last so tampering shows. CSV and PDF inspection packs, the FSAI Safe Catering record books filled in for you, and a manager's sign-off.
- **The pass and stations**: every open order on one screen, courses held until the pass fires them, and each station's queue.
- **Cook scheduler**: tells each station what to start and when, so a table's food reaches the pass together without overloading the grill or fryer, and always says why.
- **Learned cook times**: Kishtin learns how long each dish really takes; a manager can have new orders use those times.
- **Voice control**: push-to-talk commands such as "fire table 4 mains" or "walk-in fridge 3 degrees". Speech is turned into text on your own computer.
- **Orders from your till**: Square orders and paid SumUp sales come into the kitchen by themselves. Kishtin only reads from the till and never sees card details.
- **Tablets and phones**: connect each one by scanning a QR code. Staff log in with a PIN.
- **Reports**: pass times, late courses and why, and HACCP checks done and missed.
- **Backups** every 6 hours, verified after each one.

## Before you rely on it

- **This is a first release.** It has been tested with invented kitchens, not yet in a working restaurant. Run it alongside your paper records at first.
- **Copy the backups somewhere else.** They are made on the same computer; if its disk fails, they go with it.
- **On Windows and Mac, Kishtin starts when someone logs in**, not at power-on, unless the computer logs in by itself.
- **The programs are not signed** by Apple or Microsoft. The install lines above avoid the warnings a browser download may show.
- **Not tested on real hardware yet**: Windows and Mac start-up (tested only in automated checks), real iPads and Android tablets, and real Square and SumUp accounts.
- Ask your environmental health officer whether they accept Kishtin's printed or on-screen records in place of the paper booklets.
